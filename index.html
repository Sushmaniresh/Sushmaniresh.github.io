02 — How I work

Four rules I carry from project to project.

The schema is the contract.

LLM output goes into a live product only as schema-constrained JSON. Pydantic models, structured outputs, and regression tests built from real failure modes: truncation, dropped delimiters, hallucinated codes.

Measure what matters.

On imbalanced medical data, accuracy lies. I select on macro-F1 and AUC-PR, then check saliency maps and Grad-CAM++ to confirm the model is looking at the physiology, not the noise.

Small models, real devices.

A 377-parameter CNN that hears a wake word offline. A MobileNetV2 fine-tuned for on-device iOS inference. The constraint is the brief, not the obstacle.

Find the data bug first.

When a 99% model underperformed in the real world, the answer was collector bias in the dataset, not the architecture. Retraining on multi-collector data fixed it. The model is rarely the problem; the pipeline usually is.

Research Assistant · Jan – Oct 2026

Fine-tuned an ImageNet-pretrained, two-head MobileNetV2 in PyTorch to read pH and lactate from a microfluidic sensor for on-device iOS inference: 99.3% accuracy on both on a held-out set; augmentation raised both-correct accuracy from 90.7% to 98.5%.
